Discover Kashiwara — Countryside Living Close to Osaka

Experience a quieter side of Osaka in Kashiwara, where green mountains, local history, and peaceful neighbourhoods meet convenient access to the city. From Aotani, you can step onto the JR Yamatoji Line (JR大和路線 河内堅上駅) in two minutes for a ~25-minute direct ride to Tennoji, or cycle car-free along the Yamato River and Japan Heritage trails.

East to Sango & Oji (~4.5 km)

Cross to the north bank at JR Kawachi-Katakami Station and ride the paved Japan Heritage Tatsuta Kodo (龍田古道) through Kamenose and Toge into Sango (~2.4 km), then along the Yamato River across Tamon Bridge to JR Oji Station (Seiyu Oji, Super Yaohiko, Lucky Oji)—avoiding Route 25 traffic.

West to Kawachi-Kokubu (~3.6 km)

Flat, easy downstream ride along Prefectural Route 183 (~3.6–4.2 km) to everyday supermarkets and shopping: Life Kokubu, Shokuhinkan Apro Kokubu, and Gyomu Super Kashiwara Katayama.

Nearby Parks & Mountain Trails

Kamenose / Tatsuta Kodo trails (~1 km), Tatsuta Kodo Satoyama Park (~2 km uphill), Tamateyama Park (~4 km west), and Yamatogawa Kasenjiki Ryokuchi riverside park (~4.5 km west).
