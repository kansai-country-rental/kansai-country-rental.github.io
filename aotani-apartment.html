Shopping & Restaurants
Aotani is a quiet rural area with no shops or restaurants immediately next to the apartment. The closest hub is Oji, Nara (search "Oji Nara", not Oji Tokyo)—a pleasant regional center with supermarkets, shops, and restaurants just 2 stops (approx. 7 mins) away on the JR Yamatoji Line, with regular trains from early morning to late evening (approx. 3–7 trains/hour). Kawachi-Kokubu is also easily reached in the opposite direction (approx. 15 mins via Takaida + walk, by bicycle, or via Kashiwara's free weekday Kirameki loop bus) for additional supermarkets and dining.
Living in Japan
If you are unfamiliar with Japan, we are happy to assist with house-related matters like paying bills and garbage disposal, and can provide general advice on living in Japan.
Owner & Parking
Direct owners (foreigners welcome). Paid monthly parking available directly across the street at Aotani Golf Garden.
